# BrandProof Product Roadmap

This roadmap outlines planned capabilities, architectural enhancements, and ecosystem integrations beyond Round 3. Items are prioritized based on enterprise compliance demand and operational ROI.

---

## Priority 1: Enterprise Video & Multi-Frame Compliance Analyzer
- **Target Audience**: Social Media Teams, Video Editors, Brand Guardians
- **Estimated Effort**: 3 Weeks
- **Key Deliverables**:
  - Keyframe extraction and automated subtitle/closed-captioning OCR.
  - On-screen logo safe-zone and aspect ratio compliance checks.
  - Frame-by-frame WCAG contrast and flashing rate analysis for seizure-prevention (WCAG 2.3.1).
  - Integration with YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram Reels draft APIs.

---

## Priority 2: Native CMS & Design Tool Integrations (Figma & WordPress)
- **Target Audience**: Content Creators, Visual Designers
- **Estimated Effort**: 2 Weeks
- **Key Deliverables**:
  - **Figma Plugin**: Live compliance sidebar checking vector typography, contrast, and color palette tokens directly in Figma design files.
  - **Chrome Extension / CMS Integration**: Real-time linting in WordPress, Contentful, and HubSpot WYSIWYG editors without manual copy-pasting.

---

## Priority 3: Automated Regulatory Rule Packs (FDA, FTC, ASCI, GDPR)
- **Target Audience**: Legal & Regulatory Affairs, Enterprise Compliance Officers
- **Estimated Effort**: 4 Weeks
- **Key Deliverables**:
  - Pre-built, jurisdiction-specific compliance packs (e.g., FTC endorsement guidelines, ASCI India claims guidelines, FDA nutrition claim standards).
  - Automated citation of external statutory rules alongside internal brand guidelines.
  - One-click rule updates when regulatory bodies issue revised guidance.

---

## Priority 4: Decentralized Audit Chain Anchor (OpenTimestamps)
- **Target Audience**: Chief Compliance Officers, External Auditors
- **Estimated Effort**: 1 Week
- **Key Deliverables**:
  - Anchor the daily merkle root of the BrandProof SHA-256 hash chain into a public ledger (e.g., OpenTimestamps / Bitcoin blockchain).
  - Provide mathematically irrefutable timestamp proofs for legal disputes and regulatory audits.

---

## Priority 5: Advanced Localization & Multi-Language Claim Verification
- **Target Audience**: Global Marketing Organizations
- **Estimated Effort**: 3 Weeks
- **Key Deliverables**:
  - Support for multi-lingual claims verification across Spanish, German, French, Hindi, and Japanese.
  - Region-specific factual ledgers with country-specific clinical trial claims and trademark registries.
